Guidelines for Generating Technical Document with AI support*
Utilize AI for Information Gathering: Students can use AI to collect relevant data and technical details for documentation; however, the information should be articulated by them according to the flow of work and the structure of the document.
Verify Accuracy and Reliability: AI may produce inaccurate or biased information. Always verify the facts and relevance of AI-generated content before including it in the final documentation.
Fact-Check AI Content: Double-check AI-generated content for factual accuracy by validating data, claims, and conclusions through trusted and reliable sources to maintain the document's integrity.
Follow Citation Guidelines: Students must properly cite all external sources, whether referred by them or AI, following the required citation style.

Sprint Functional Document for Health and Fitness app
1. Introduction
The Health and Fitness App project focuses on enhancing the user experience by introducing personalized workout plans and nutrition tracking. Sprint 2 (example) aims to implement advanced features such as real-time workout tracking and integration with wearable devices.
2. Product Goal
The primary goal of this sprint is to elevate the health and fitness app's functionality by incorporating real-time workout tracking capabilities and seamless integration with popular wearable devices. This aligns with the overarching project objective of providing users with a comprehensive and personalized fitness experience.
3. Demography (Users, Location)
Users
Target Users: Fitness enthusiasts, casual users, and nutrition-conscious individuals
User Characteristics: Diverse fitness goals, various fitness levels
Location
Target Location: Global usage with a focus on health-conscious communities
4. Business Processes
Key business processes include:
User Onboarding:
Process for users to create profiles and set fitness goals.
Workout Planning:
Process for users to receive personalized workout plans.
Nutrition Tracking:
Process for users to log and track their daily nutrition intake.
5. Features
This sprint will focus on implementing the following key features:
Real-time Workout Tracking:
Integrate sensors for real-time tracking of exercises.
Provide instant feedback on form and performance.
Wearable Device Integration:
Enable seamless synchronization with popular wearable devices.
Utilize device data for more accurate fitness insights.
